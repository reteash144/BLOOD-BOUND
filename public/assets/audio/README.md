# Background music

Place your soundtrack here as `bloodbound-theme.mp4`. The game loads it from
`/assets/audio/bloodbound-theme.mp4` and loops it during gameplay.

For broad browser compatibility, use an MP4 container with an AAC-LC audio
track (no video track is required). Playback begins after the player clicks
**INITIALIZE OPERATIVE**, respects the MUSIC ON/OFF control, and pauses when
the game is paused or returned to the title screen.
